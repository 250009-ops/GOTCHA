## One-page memo (read this first)

**GOTCHA is a live, reading-first language-learning platform.** Learners become fluent by reading real books and articles — not by tapping cartoon drills. They tap any word for an AI definition in context, save it into spaced-repetition vocabulary, hear TTS, take AI quizzes on the same passage, and import their own PDF / EPUB / URL / paste. Catalog titles can be free or paid. Imports are **pay-as-you-go in Uzbek soʻm** through Atmos (UzCard, Humo, Visa, Mastercard). There is **no subscription**.

**It is already in production in Uzbekistan** on a real domain, with a real payment rail, a real admin CMS, a native Android app, and a desktop learner web app. Live languages today: **English, Russian, Uzbek**. The same admin panel already contains a **40-language world catalog**; turning a language on is an operational switch, not a rewrite.

**What is not true yet:** this is not a scaled consumer business. The public marketing API reported **15 learners** on 19 August 2026. The catalog is a curated trilingual library (classic and modern literature, CEFR A1–C2), not millions of titles. The stack runs on **one VPS** in Uzbekistan. iOS is not shipped. Google Play listing is not complete. International cards beyond Atmos, and global tax/billing, are not built.

**Why an international investor should care anyway:** most language apps fail in emerging markets because they have no local books, no local cards, and a Western subscription. GOTCHA already solved those three on a hard beachhead (Uzbek + Russian + English, local acquiring). The software to go global is largely in place. Capital is required for **catalog depth at world scale, infrastructure, iOS + store distribution, and international payments** — the things a small team cannot buy with time alone.

**How it was built:** Bootstrapped to production. No prior institutional round.

**The raise:** Seed round — **amount not printed on the deck** (discussed in the room). Purpose: **hire the team** and take the live platform **global in 3 months**. Payments in Uzbekistan: **Atmos FinTech merchant agreement through 2031**. No tech-stack slide. No use-of-funds percentages.

### 07 — How it works

Three steps, then the loop:

1. **Choose a book** from the library, or drop a PDF / EPUB / URL / paste.
2. **Read.** Tap a word or phrase → AI meaning in context → optional save → TTS.
3. **Keep it.** SRS review + quizzes generated from the chapter you just read.

**Commerce loop:** Free allowance → quote by page count → Atmos card + OTP → import proceeds. Catalog paywall for paid titles (hard paywall; no “first chapter free”).

**Admin loop:** Ingest preview (file or text) → chapters/pages/tokens → draft → license/moderation → publish → learner feed.
