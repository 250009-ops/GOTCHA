We refund a pass only if the purchase is **faulty**, which means one of these: **charged but not delivered** (you paid, but the pass was not unlocked); **broken content** (Plus does not work as described and we can't fix it within a reasonable time); a **failed charge** (the payment failed, but money was still taken); or a **duplicate charge** (you were charged twice for the same pass). Write to **support@gotchaa.uz** within **14 calendar days** of purchase. Give your account email, the payment date and what went wrong. We first try to fix the problem. If we can't, we refund the payment to the same card through Atmos within **14 days** of accepting your claim, and the Plus access ends.

**Cancellation requests** also go to **support@gotchaa.uz**. We review each request. Nothing is cancelled or refunded automatically. A pass that was delivered and works as described is not refunded, for example if you change your mind, unless the law requires otherwise. **Nothing in this offer limits your rights under the consumer protection laws of Uzbekistan.**
