Gotcha Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data Gotcha collects, why, where it is stored, who we share it with, how long we keep it and what rights you have. It follows the Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan No. ZRU-547 "On personal data".

1. Who we are

Gotcha is run by the Erkin Tizim team (the data controller). In this policy "Gotcha", "we" and "us" mean the Erkin Tizim team. You can reach us at support@gotchaa.uz.

This policy applies to the Gotcha website gotchaa.uz and the web app gotchaa.uz/learn (together, the "Service").

2. Personal data we collect

You give us:

  • Account data: email address, password (stored only as a one-way hash), display name, native language, interface language, the languages you learn, time zone and preferences (such as daily goal and theme).
  • Age: your year of birth, which we ask once, after you sign up. Giving it is optional; if you skip it, we treat your account as that of a 13–17-year-old (section 12).
  • Profile photo: if you choose to upload one. Children under 13 cannot upload one.
  • Learning data: reading progress and sessions, saved and known words, review history, quiz and practice answers, streaks and other stats.
  • Imported content: texts you paste, web links you submit (our server fetches the page), and PDF and EPUB files you upload, together with the text extracted from them.
  • Messages: messages you send to other learners, including edits and replies.
  • Reports and blocks: reports you make about users or messages, and users you block.
  • Support requests: what you write to support@gotchaa.uz.

We create or receive while you use the Service:

  • Payment data: payment ID, amount, status, dates, the fiscal receipt link and refund details. See section 6 for how card details are handled.
  • Access records: which imports and books you have unlocked.
  • Usage counters: for example, daily AI lookups and free imports, so we can apply the free allowance.
  • Presence: whether you are online, when you were last seen, and typing and read indicators in Messages.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type and version, kept in our server logs for security and troubleshooting.
  • Consent records: what you agreed to, which version and when.

We do not collect your location, contacts, microphone audio or biometric data. We do not use third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

4. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on our servers in Uzbekistan, hosted by Eskiz. Backups are also kept in Uzbekistan. The main copy of your data always stays in Uzbekistan. The only processing outside Uzbekistan is the AI processing described in section 5.

5. AI processing by OpenAI (USA)

Gotcha's AI features use models from OpenAI, L.L.C. in the USA. They are available to users of all ages. AI features, including word lookup, are on for every account, whatever your age: every lookup, translation, quiz, import clean-up and audio request is sent to OpenAI as described below.

What is sent. AI features send OpenAI the text the feature needs, plus both languages (the one you are learning and your own), together with our instructions to the model. Depending on the feature, this can include the sentence around a word you tap, the full text of a chat message you translate, which may be a message another user wrote to you, and the content and titles of documents you import. They send no account identifiers: none of our AI requests includes your user ID, name or email, or OpenAI's "user" field.

Feature What is sent
Word lookup The word you tap, the sentence around it, and both languages. This also works inside chat messages, so a sentence from a message you received can be sent.
Translation The full text of the chat message you choose to translate (which may be a message another user wrote), and both languages
Text-to-speech (audio) The text to be read aloud (up to 400 characters, which can be a word or chat text) and, for some voices, a label saying which language it is in
Chapter quiz Up to 4,000 characters of the passage the quiz is about
Import clean-up The content of the PDF, EPUB or web page you import, plus the document and chapter titles, so that non-reading parts can be removed. Long books and Project Gutenberg imports skip this step.

Never sent: your name, email, password, year of birth, account ID, payment details or photo. Text recognition in scanned PDFs runs on our own server.

Messages you send. The people you chat with can use word lookup and translation on messages you send them. When they do, the text of your message (or the sentence around the word they tap) is sent to OpenAI as described above, without your name or account details.

Extra safety steps for under-18s and accounts without an age. If your account belongs to someone under 18, or no year of birth was given, two extra things happen:

  • Age-appropriate instruction: word lookups, translations and quizzes include an instruction telling the model that the learner may be a child or teenager and that its output must be age-appropriate. This tells OpenAI the age group only; your year of birth is never sent.
  • OpenAI moderation: translations (including saved ones) and generated quizzes are checked with OpenAI's moderation service before you see them (up to 4,000 characters per check). A flagged translation is replaced with "[hidden for safety]", and a flagged quiz is not shown. If the moderation service is unavailable, the result is not shown; you can try again later. Word lookups, imports and audio are not moderated.

These steps reduce the risk of unsuitable content but cannot guarantee that every output is suitable.

Turning AI off. You can turn AI features off for your account in Settings. While AI is off, your account sends nothing to OpenAI: word lookup, translation, quizzes, import clean-up and text-to-speech stop working for you. You can turn AI back on at any time, unless our support team turned it off for your account; in that case write to support@gotchaa.uz.

Please don't upload or type personal data. A document you import is sent as it is, so it may contain whatever you put in it. Don't import documents, or type answers or select text, that contain your own or other people's personal data (for example names, phone numbers, addresses or ID numbers).

Purpose and retention. The text is used only to produce the result you asked for. Word explanations and translations may be cached on our server so they can be reused; these caches are not linked to your account. A translation of a chat message is also saved with that message in the conversation. Text-to-speech audio is stored on our server: each text you ask to hear is saved as an audio file in Uzbekistan so it can be replayed; these files are deleted after 30 days by a daily cleanup job. Apart from these caches, saved translations and audio files, we don't log the AI's responses. OpenAI does not use this text to train its models and does not retain it (zero data retention).

Transfer to the USA and your consent. This text is processed in the USA, which may not ensure the same level of data protection as Uzbekistan. The legal basis for this transfer is the consent you give at sign-up, when you tick the box agreeing to the Terms of Use and this Privacy Policy, including this transfer. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your account (section 11) or by writing to support@gotchaa.uz. AI features then stop working for your account; everything else in Gotcha keeps working. Withdrawal does not affect text already processed.

6. Payments

Payments on gotchaa.uz are processed by Atmos with Uzcard and Humo cards. You enter your card number and expiry date in the payment form. They are sent over an encrypted connection to Atmos for that payment, and Atmos sends a confirmation code to the phone number linked to your card. We do not store your full card number. A fiscal receipt is issued electronically through the fiscal data operator (OFD).

7. Who we share data with

We share personal data only with these providers, and only as far as they need it:

  • Eskiz (Uzbekistan): hosting, storage, backups and email;
  • OpenAI, L.L.C. (USA): AI processing of the text a feature needs (a tapped word with its sentence, chat messages you translate, quiz passages, imported documents and their titles, text to be read aloud), with no account identifiers, based on the consent given at sign-up (section 5);
  • Atmos and the fiscal data operator (Uzbekistan): payments and fiscal receipts.

Other users see your display name, photo, the languages you learn, your online status and last-seen time, and the messages you send them. You may appear in the learner directory and in suggestions. Minors are not listed in the learner directory or in suggestions (section 12).

We may disclose data to state bodies when Uzbek law requires it, or to protect the safety of users and the Service. If the Service is transferred to another person or company, your data may pass to them, and they will be bound by this policy.

9. Messages, reporting and blocking

Messages are visible only to you and the person you are writing to. We don't read them, except when a message is reported, when we need to investigate abuse or a security issue, or when the law requires it. You can edit and delete your own messages.

You can report a message or a user, and block a user so they can no longer message you. We review reports and may remove content, warn users, or suspend or close accounts that break the Terms of Use. We never tell a reported user who reported them.

Imported texts and files are private to your account.

10. Your rights

Under ZRU-547 you have the right to:

  • know whether we process your data, why and how;
  • access your data and get a copy;
  • correct it (most details can be changed in Settings);
  • have it deleted or destroyed (section 11);
  • withdraw consent that you have given (if you withdraw consent to the OpenAI transfer, AI features stop, section 5; you can also turn AI off in Settings);
  • object to processing or ask us to restrict it;
  • complain to the authorised state body for personal data or to a court.

To use a right, email support@gotchaa.uz from your account email. We may ask you to confirm your identity, and we reply within the period set by law.

11. Deleting your account

You can delete your Gotcha account at any time.

How to delete your account:

  • On the website: sign in at gotchaa.uz/learn, open Settings → Delete account, and confirm with your password.
  • By email: write to support@gotchaa.uz from your account email with the subject "Delete my account". We may ask you to confirm.
  • Parents and guardians can ask us to delete a minor's account in the same way (section 12).

What happens:

  • You are signed out everywhere straight away, and the account can no longer be used.
  • Deleted (we start straight away and aim to finish within 30 days; if a step fails, we fix it and complete the deletion):
  • your profile (email, name, photo), preferences and languages;
  • reading progress, saved and known words, and review history;
  • practice and quiz results;
  • imports and their files;
  • notifications, blocks and stats.
  • Anonymised, not removed: messages you sent to other users stay in their conversations, so that the conversations still make sense. They are shown as from "Deleted user", and your name, email and photo are removed. Internally they stay attached to that anonymised account record. If you want particular messages gone, delete them before you delete your account.
  • Kept:
  • payment, refund and fiscal records that the law requires us to keep, without your learning data;
  • reports about rule breaches, with any message attached to them, until about 1 year after the report is closed (longer only if needed for an ongoing dispute or a request from a state body);
  • copies in backups, until they are overwritten.
  • Purchases are tied to your account and can't be restored after deletion.

12. Children and minors

Gotcha has no minimum age.

Users under 18. Under Uzbek law (ZRU-547, Article 21), anyone under 18 is a minor. We ask for your year of birth once, after you sign up; if you skip it, we don't ask again. Once given, it can be changed only by writing to support@gotchaa.uz. Giving it is optional, but if no year of birth is given, we treat the account as that of a 13–17-year-old, and all the protections for under-18s below apply.

Children under 13: additional rules.

  • We collect only what is needed for learning: a nickname (no real name needed), age, languages, progress, saved words and settings. Children cannot upload a profile photo.
  • There are no ads and no behavioural profiling.

Extra protections for everyone under 18:

  • Messaging:
  • Children under 13 cannot use Messages.
  • 13–17-year-olds can message only accepted friends: one user sends a friend request, the other accepts it, and only then can they write to each other. Either side can remove the friendship or block at any time.
  • Minors are not listed in the learner directory or in suggestions.
  • AI: AI features are available to all ages, including children under 13. The rules in section 5 apply, including the extra safety steps for under-18s: the model is told the learner may be a child or teenager, and translations and quizzes are checked with OpenAI moderation. The child's name, email, year of birth and account identifiers are never sent.
  • Imports from web links: minors can import web pages and files like everyone else. We may filter or block content that is unsuitable for minors. What a user imports remains their own responsibility, and we recommend that parents supervise their children's imports. Anyone can use the Report button to tell us about unsuitable content.

Parents' and guardians' rights. A parent or guardian can:

  • see their child's data and get a copy;
  • correct it;
  • have it deleted;
  • stop further collection.

To do any of this, write to support@gotchaa.uz with the subject "Parent request" and give the child's account email. We will check that you are the parent or guardian before acting.

13. Cookies and browser storage

We use only the browser storage needed for the Service to work: keeping you signed in and remembering your language and theme. We use no analytics or advertising cookies.

14. Security

All data is sent over HTTPS, and passwords are stored only as hashes. Only authorised people can access our servers, and only as far as their work needs. If a breach affects your data, we act as ZRU-547 requires, including stopping the processing or destroying the affected data and notifying you and the authorities where required.

15. Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, we change the effective date. If the changes are material, we tell you by email or in the Service before they take effect, and where the law requires it we ask for your consent again.

16. Language versions

This policy is published in Uzbek, Russian and English. If the versions differ, the Uzbek text prevails.
